# Content Strategy: Offer, Educate, Tell Stories

## The core idea

All our marketing content is one of three types: **sales**, **education**, or **story**. Education and story build trust between offers, and offers turn that trust into revenue. We run this cycle every month and improve our execution each time.

## Monthly rhythm

- **Two offers per month.** Each offer is a launch plus one reminder. We publish no other sales content.
- **The days between offers** go to education and stories.
- **Plan backward from each offer.** Before scheduling anything, ask what the audience needs to know and feel before this offer lands.

## 1. Sales content

**Purpose:** convert the trust we've built.

1. **Keep the offer simple.** Use percentage off, dollar amount off, free shipping, buy-one-get-one, or bundle-and-save.
2. **Always give a reason.** People buy on emotion and justify with logic, and the reason supplies the logic. Good reasons include a calendar date, excess stock, or a milestone. A playful reason works if it fits the brand.
3. **Respect the cadence.** Selling more often trades long-term audience goodwill for short-term revenue. Stay at two offers a month.

## 2. Educational content

**Purpose:** make us the authority the audience trusts, so offers convert.

**Choosing topics:**
1. List the problems our product solves.
2. For each problem, ask what the customer must understand before our product is clearly the best solution.
3. Each answer becomes a piece of content.

**Structure:** Present every piece as a clear sequence of steps or points, such as "3 steps to…" or "How X is made." The title doesn't need to be a list, but the content should follow one.

**Rule:** Education published before an offer should set up that specific offer.

## 3. Story

**Purpose:** make both sales and education more effective. Build stories into those pieces rather than treating story only as a separate content slot.

**Structure:** intent → obstacle → resolution.

**Template:**
- "We set out to give you **[mission / benefit]**."
- "But **[problem]** got in the way."
- "So we **[sacrifice or decision]**, because giving you **[mission / benefit]** matters that much to us."

**Framing:** The brand mission is the series, and each email, post, or ad is an episode that moves it forward.

**Where stories come from:** our mission, brand values, product values, and what we stand against.

**Strongest stories:** those where our values are tested while we solve the problem our product addresses.

## Pre-publish check

Every piece must pass all three:

1. **Relevant:** Is it useful, interesting, or important to the audience?
2. **Closer:** Does it bring people closer to the brand and product?
3. **Connected:** Which offer does it lead toward?

If a piece fails any of these, rework it or cut it.
